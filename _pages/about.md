# About me

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am supervised by **Professor Junxian He**. I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024.

My research sits at the intersection of **natural language processing** and **machine learning**, with a focus on large language model reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

# Academic background
