I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am a member of the **HKUST NLP Group** led by Prof. Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. degree from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, and I worked with Prof. Junxian He since my undergraduate studies there.

My research lies at the intersection of **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. I am interested in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)**, and the **truthfulness and interpretability of large language models**.

A condensed version of the information below is also available on my [CV]({{ root | append: "/cv/" }}), while the full publication list is available on the [Publications]({{ root | append: "/publications/" }}) page.
